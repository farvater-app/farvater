Farvater connects your phone to your own server or to one from a subscription and routes all app traffic through it. All you need is a subscription link: the app finds the fastest responding node by itself. Russian government, banking, Yandex and VK services are routed directly.

<b>Connection</b>
<ul>
<li>One-tap connect with automatic selection of the fastest node.</li>
<li>The selected node never changes by itself: if it stops responding, the app tells you.</li>
<li>Speed, traffic per session and per day, session time and transport security on the home screen.</li>
</ul>

<b>Subscriptions</b>
<ul>
<li>Your own subscriptions by URL and single nodes from the clipboard.</li>
<li>Whitelist and blacklist network modes: each subscription has its own mode, and the Auto mode detects network restrictions by itself.</li>
<li>Per-subscription update interval.</li>
<li>Optional HWID for panels that limit the number of devices, sent only to your own subscriptions.</li>
</ul>

<b>Routing</b>
<ul>
<li>Russian services and local networks bypass the tunnel.</li>
<li>Any app can be excluded from the VPN.</li>
<li>IPv6 is blocked, DNS goes over DoH inside the tunnel by default.</li>
</ul>

<b>Appearance</b>
<ul>
<li>Four styles to choose from: iOS 26 with Liquid Glass, Material You with wallpaper colors, #RosKomPozor in #RKP colors, or Sumi-e: ink on rice paper and a cat in a boat.</li>
<li>Light, dark or system theme.</li>
</ul>

<b>Security</b>
<ul>
<li>The local proxy listens on 127.0.0.1 only and requires a random password.</li>
<li>No analytics, ads or trackers.</li>
<li>Updates are verified by SHA-256 and APK signature.</li>
</ul>

Protocols: VLESS (including Reality and XHTTP), VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, Hysteria2. Core: Xray-core, tunnel: hev-socks5-tunnel.

Farvater does not provide any servers. The source code is available under GPL-3.0. The interface is in Russian.
